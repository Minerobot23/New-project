# Content rules (apply to every agent and skill)

Fluxline Solutions is a real business. Never fabricate anything presented as fact:

- No invented clients, testimonials, reviews, ratings, case studies, results, statistics, certifications, awards, addresses, phone numbers, or business history.
- No guarantees of rankings, traffic, leads, or conversions, and no fake urgency or scarcity.
- Demo businesses in the simulator and on /work are fictional and must stay labeled "Interactive Concept Demo" or "Concept Project".
- Photos must be properly licensed (see docs/PHOTO-CREDITS.md); record the source of any new photo there.
- Never commit .env files, API keys, or other secrets, and never expose secrets client-side.
